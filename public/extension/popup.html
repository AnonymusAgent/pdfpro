Edit, convert, OCR and sign PDFs. Files open in the PDF Pro editor tab and are processed in your browser.

This site's PDFs: PDF Pro needs your permission to download PDF links from this site when you use the right-click menu.

What permissions does PDF Pro use?

contextMenus adds “Open in PDF Pro” to links and PDF pages.
storage saves the PDF Pro web address.
activeTab lets this popup read the current tab's address when you click the toolbar icon.
Optional site access is requested only when you click “Allow access”, for the site you choose. PDF Pro does not read pages, scripts or content of websites, and cannot access browser-internal pages.
